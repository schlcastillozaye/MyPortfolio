✿01 — about me

A little more about me.

The person behind the screens, layouts, and occasional overthinking.

Hi, I'm Zaye! I'm a third-year Information Technology student at Bulacan State University, and I spend a lot of time learning new things, working on school projects, and figuring out how to turn the ideas in my head into something real.

I'm especially drawn to the design side of things. I enjoy experimenting with layouts, choosing colors, fixing little details, and making interfaces feel cohesive. I can spend a surprising amount of time tweaking something small just because I feel like it could look better.

I also enjoy the technical side of it — writing code, figuring out how things work, and solving problems when something doesn't go as planned. I like that I get to be both creative and logical, and that there's always something new to learn.

I'm still growing as a designer and developer, so I don't expect myself to know everything yet. I'd rather keep experimenting, improve through my projects, and learn from the things that don't work the first time.

For me, the best part is seeing something go from an idea to an actual working project, especially when it looks good and serves a purpose, too.

— Zaye ♡

RoadWatch is a road issue reporting and maintenance management system. Citizens can report road problems, while administrators and maintenance officers review the reports, assign work, and keep track of repairs.

My part

I worked on this as part of a BSIT group project at Bulacan State University, with a focus on testing. My role was to look at the different user flows, check that things behaved as expected, and help spot issues along the way.

Built with

PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and Flutter — with a web app and a mobile app sharing one backend.
